Focus

Your next task, on every screen.

A small widget that floats above every window with your tasks and one bold timer. Stop checking your phone to know what is next.

Download for Windows Mac

Free. About 110 MB. Updates install on their own.

One more click on Windows. Open the file you just downloaded. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Focus installs in seconds and appears in the top-right corner of your screen.
One more step on Mac. Open the file and drag Focus into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may block it. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Always in viewStays on top of your browser, your editor and your calls. Drag it, resize it, or tuck it into the screen edge.
One timer, real timesStart, pause and tick tasks off. Every session is logged with the clock time it happened.
Yours to styleYour own photo, gradients, button colours, light or dark. Your data stays on your computer.
